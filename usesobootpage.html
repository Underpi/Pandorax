About this bootcamp

This bootcamp prepares students for the first round of the US Earth Science Olympiad, assuming no prior experience with Earth science. This bootcamp covers all four spheres of Earth: geosphere, hydrosphere, atmosphere, and astronomy. By the end of the bootcamp, students should have enough knowledge to reach the national training camp of the USESO.

Cost

Free. Unlike our other Olympiad bootcamps, this one is offered at no charge — there is nothing to pay to take part.

Prerequisites

None, but a basic understanding of high school algebra is recommended.
