Machine Learning Research Bootcamp

Schedule 12 classes · 8/15 – 8/29, daily 7–8 PM EST

About this bootcamp

Machine learning (ML) and AI is a rapidly advancing field of science, but just knowing ML/AI is not enough — you must know how to use it. This bootcamp will teach you the fundamentals of AI as well as advanced ML. You will experience conducting AI/ML research. By the end of the bootcamp, you would have produced a high-end ML model for a research project of your own. With newfound knowledge from this valuable opportunity, you will be equipped with the tools to continue pursuing AI/ML and compete in and win science fairs, such as the prestigious Regeneron ISEF.

Prerequisites

Python knowledge is not required; however, some programming experience is recommended.
