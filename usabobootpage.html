About this bootcamp

NOTE: this is an advanced USABO bootcamp. This bootcamp will prepare students for the USABO semifinal round, covering 5 important units critical in reaching top 50 and camp: Biochemistry, Cell Biology, Genetics, Plants, and Animal Physiology. By the end of the bootcamp, students should have enough knowledge to reach top 50 or higher at the USABO semifinal round.
