Bootcamps

For those who are curious enough to dive even deeper and attend live lectures by some of the most accomplished young scientists in the world, we proudly offer our bootcamps.

Each bootcamp is taught by a team of internationally-recognized instructors, who hold numerous accolades from numerous international science Olympiads and/or the International Science & Engineering Fair (ISEF). For the most curious of them all, our bootcamps are for you to explore.
